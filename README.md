**Solmar Bay — Sun, Sand & Sirens** is an original open-world crime game in full 3D, built for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty. Mara Vega, getaway driver for The Harbor Crew, runs eight story jobs across a sun-soaked coastal city at golden hour — stealing cars, outrunning the SBPD, and facing down the Coral Kings — while the whole city watches from the sidewalks.

Everything is original: the city, the characters, the story, the art, and the engine. The game is raw WebGL written from scratch (own matrix math, shaders and meshes — no third-party libraries), and it plays entirely offline from static files.

## What's in the city

- **A living 3D world**: ~100 buildings with lit windows, glass downtown towers with rooftop props, palm-lined beach and ocean, a working port with cranes and container stacks, a marina with moored boats, streetlights, neon signs, fog and a golden-hour sky with a low sun.
- **Three cameras**: a slow downtown orbit on the title screen, a chase cam behind the car, and an over-shoulder cam on foot.
- **Eight story missions** with stages, timers and dialogue from Reyes and Juno — car theft, timed deliveries, a harbor rescue, a convoy hit, a port heist, and two survive-the-heat finales up to 5 stars.
- **Six stealable car types** (sports, sedan, SUV, taxi, truck, police) with arcade handling, damage, smoke and fire.
- **A full wanted system**: 1–5 stars, chasing and ramming cruisers, foot cops from 2 stars, a **police helicopter with a visible spotlight** from 3 stars, roadblocks from 4 stars, heat decay, and a busted screen with a fine.
- **Combat**: fists, pistol and SMG with ammo, reload and mouse aim; hostile Coral Kings rivals and armed cops shoot back.
- **Dense GTA-style HUD**: mission panel with stage and next-step hint, live SBPD dispatch list, weapon slots, cash, clock, wanted stars, rank and XP bar, radio ticker, round live minimap, district label, health/armour bars, and a speedometer with vehicle condition.
- **Extras**: 20 AI traffic cars, 40 fleeing pedestrians, health/armour/cash pickups, localStorage save, and synthesized WebAudio SFX with an ambient loop — zero network requests.

## Controls

WASD drive & walk · E enter / exit car · Mouse aim · Click shoot · R reload · 1/2/3 weapons · Space handbrake · Shift sprint · J start job · P pause · H help

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — no build step, no install, no network.
